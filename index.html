Nalanda Mahavihara

History & Significance

Nalanda was a renowned mahavihara (ancient residential Buddhist university) in Bihar, India. Considered to be the world's first residential university, it flourished under the Gupta Empire and Emperor Harsha.

auto_awesome Cultural Impact

At its peak, it attracted scholars from Tibet, China, Korea, and Central Asia, serving as a pivotal center for the transmission of Buddhist philosophy.

wb_sunny Chhath Puja: Worshipping the Sun

Chhath Puja is an ancient Hindu festival deeply rooted in expressing gratitude to Surya, the Sun God, and Chhathi Maiya, for sustaining life on earth. It is celebrated with profound devotion, particularly in the Indian states of Bihar, Jharkhand, and Uttar Pradesh, as well as regions of Nepal.

Unlike many other festivals, Chhath is unique because it doesn't involve idol worship. Instead, devotees offer prayers directly to the setting and rising sun, standing waist-deep in water—usually a river or pond.
